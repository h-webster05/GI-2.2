name: "cedar-ridge-noi-and-cap-rate"
description: "solve for the noi and cap rate from a rent roll and opex summary.. Make sure to account for vacancy, non revenue units and concessions"
---

Read rent roll and opex sheet throughout. Make sure to read notes on bottom of durham rent roll
Multiple every unit monthly rent by 12 to an annual Gross Potential Rent
Under “status” column in rent roll, if any unit is vacant, subtract its annual amount from total
Read notes section and any unit is occupied by on site manager as part of their compensation, subtract the annual unit amount from the total as thats non revenue unit
Read notes section and subtract any concessions from that specific units annual rent. For example is 1 month free rent is subtracting the annual by 1 month rent to get effective rental income
Look through opex statement for other income and add the total to what we have so for to get effective gross rent
Subtract total annual operating expensises to get noi
To find the cap rate, look for the asking price on the rent roll. You are to take your noi and divide it by the asking price/ sale price to get cap rate
